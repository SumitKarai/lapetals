About Rajrajeshwari Lifescience Pvt. Ltd

Operating under the trusted brand name La Petals, Rajrajeshwari Lifescience Pvt. Ltd is a forward-thinking Ayurvedic formulation company. We are dedicated to bridging the timeless wisdom of traditional herbal heritage with the precision of modern nanotechnology.

Every formulation in our extensive lineup—from specialized joint care remedies and wellness supplements to post-natal tonics, skincare, and haircare—is GMP Certified. We combine 100% pure, ethically sourced botanical extracts with rigorous batch control and zero harmful chemicals or toxic heavy metals.

To deliver accessible, high-purity Ayurvedic remedies that provide measurable health solutions by merging age-old botanical knowledge with cutting-edge scientific delivery methods.

Quality Standards
